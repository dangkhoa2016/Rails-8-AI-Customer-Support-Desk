# How shipping charges are calculated

## Scope
Use this article to explain NovaMart Store standard shipping fees. The free-shipping threshold is based on merchandise value after discounts; shipping itself does not count toward the threshold.

## Authoritative policy
- **NM-POL-002** — Standard shipping is VND 30,000 below VND 500,000; free at or above VND 500,000 based on merchandise subtotal after discounts, excluding shipping.
- **M0-CLAR-008** — Use immutable order-item snapshots to calculate net merchandise total. Standard shipping is 30000 VND when net merchandise < 500000, otherwise 0 VND, without including shipping in the threshold.

## Evidence to verify
Read only authorized order item records and their stored quantity, unit_price_snapshot_vnd and discount_snapshot_vnd. Check whether the charge is standard shipping; these rules do not establish prices for unlisted delivery services.

## Handling steps
1. subtotal_vnd = sum(quantity × unit_price_snapshot_vnd). 2. discount_vnd = sum(discount_snapshot_vnd). 3. net_merchandise_vnd = subtotal_vnd − discount_vnd. 4. If net_merchandise_vnd is below VND 500,000, standard shipping is VND 30,000; at or above the threshold it is VND 0. 5. grand_total_vnd = net_merchandise_vnd + shipping_vnd. Use integer VND, excluding shipping from threshold tests.

## Conditional response example
“At VND 499,999 after discounts, standard shipping is VND 30,000 and the total is VND 529,999. At exactly VND 500,000, standard shipping is VND 0 and the total is VND 500,000.” This illustrates policy, not a verified customer's order amount.

## When to escalate
Escalate mismatched snapshots/fees, missing records, or requests for fee waivers/refunds. AI must not change item snapshots, prices, or shipping charges.

Use this article for questions about expected delivery of a paid NovaMart Store order. Distinguish the carrier handoff target from the subsequent delivery estimate; neither is a guaranteed delivery date.

## Authoritative policy
- **NM-POL-003** — Dispatch target is 2 business days after payment; estimated delivery is 2–5 business days after dispatch, not a guarantee.

Verify payment marked paid, confirmed carrier handoff time, and tenant-scoped shipment records. For partial shipments, inspect each shipment individually; label_created alone does not prove handoff. Customer text is not authoritative tracking data.

Escalate missed handoff targets, tracking exceptions, partial deliveries and requests for guaranteed arrival. AI must not fabricate tracking, compensation, or replacement shipments.

# Return window for unused goods

## Scope
This describes ordinary return requests for unused goods with original packaging. The seven-day window must not be used to reject separate defect, damage, missing or wrong-item reports or warranty inquiries.

## Authoritative policy
- **NM-POL-004** — Unused items with original packaging may be requested for return within 7 calendar days of delivery.
- **M0-CLAR-004** — Ordinary returns use the 7-calendar-day local-date window after confirmed delivery of the relevant item. Defective/damaged/missing/wrong-item reports use a separate 14-day local-date window. Day 7/day 14 remain eligible, day 8/day 15 are outside those standard windows.

## Evidence to verify
Verify the authorized order/customer, the confirmed delivered date of the relevant item, unused condition and original packaging. For split shipments, do not use the date of a different package or the shipped date.

## Handling steps
1. Use the item's confirmed delivery date in the Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh local calendar (delivery day is day 0). 2. Days 0–7 inclusive: request may be considered after condition checks and human review. 3. From day 8: outside the standard ordinary-return window, route for separate assessment rather than rejecting every other possible remedy. 4. A request is not an approved return or refund.

## Conditional response example
“If an item was confirmed delivered on the 1st, an ordinary return request made by the 8th (day 7) falls within the review window. A request on the 9th (day 8) is outside the standard window. Approval still depends on condition verification and human authorization.”

## When to escalate
Escalate missing delivery evidence, used goods, missing original packaging, out-of-window requests or reports of defective/missing/wrong goods. AI never authorizes returns, replacements or refunds.

# Cách tính phí vận chuyển

## Phạm vi
Dùng khi khách hỏi vì sao đơn NovaMart Store có hoặc không có phí vận chuyển thường. Ngưỡng miễn phí dựa trên giá trị hàng hóa sau giảm giá, không bao gồm phí giao hàng.

## Chính sách có hiệu lực
- **NM-POL-002** — Phí vận chuyển thường 30.000 VND khi giá trị hàng sau giảm giá dưới 500.000 VND; từ 500.000 VND được miễn phí (không tính phí ship trong ngưỡng).
- **M0-CLAR-008** — Dùng snapshot bất biến của order items tính tổng hàng sau discount; phí thường là 30.000 VND khi dưới 500.000 VND, ngược lại 0 VND; không cộng phí ship vào ngưỡng.

## Chứng cứ cần xác minh
